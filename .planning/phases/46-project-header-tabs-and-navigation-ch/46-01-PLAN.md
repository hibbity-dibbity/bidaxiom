The 9 parallel queries (in order):
1. Accepted estimate totalCost — `db.select({ totalCost: schema.estimates.totalCost }).from(schema.estimates).where(and(eq(schema.estimates.projectId, projectId), eq(schema.estimates.status, "accepted"))).orderBy(desc(schema.estimates.createdAt)).limit(1)`
2. Sum of approved CO total_amount — `db.select({ total: sql<string>\`COALESCE(SUM(total_amount), 0)\` }).from(schema.changeOrders).where(and(eq(schema.changeOrders.projectId, projectId), eq(schema.changeOrders.status, "approved")))`
3. Project budget field — `db.select({ budget: schema.projects.budget }).from(schema.projects).where(eq(schema.projects.id, projectId)).limit(1)`
4. Billed to Date (ALL payment apps, no status filter) — `db.select({ total: sql<string>\`COALESCE(SUM(current_payment_due), 0)\` }).from(schema.paymentApps).where(eq(schema.paymentApps.projectId, projectId))`
5. Retainage held — `db.select({ total: sql<string>\`COALESCE(SUM(amount_held - COALESCE(released_amount, 0)), 0)\` }).from(schema.retainageHeld).where(eq(schema.retainageHeld.projectId, projectId))`
6. CO pending badge count — `db.select({ count: count() }).from(schema.changeOrders).where(and(eq(schema.changeOrders.projectId, projectId), inArray(schema.changeOrders.status, ["pco_submitted", "pending_approval"])))`
7. Payment apps pending badge count — `db.select({ count: count() }).from(schema.paymentApps).where(and(eq(schema.paymentApps.projectId, projectId), inArray(schema.paymentApps.status, ["submitted", "approved"]), isNull(schema.paymentApps.paidAt)))`
8. Documents count — `db.select({ count: count() }).from(schema.documents).where(eq(schema.documents.projectId, projectId))`
9. Invoices count (payment app count as AR proxy) — `db.select({ count: count() }).from(schema.paymentApps).where(eq(schema.paymentApps.projectId, projectId))`
