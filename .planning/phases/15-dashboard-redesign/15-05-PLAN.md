**Layout structure:**

```tsx
<div className="space-y-6">
  {/* Header */}
  <div>
    <h1 className="text-3xl font-bold tracking-tight">Dashboard</h1>
    <p className="text-muted-foreground">Welcome to {companyName}</p>
  </div>

  {/* User-Level Widgets - "My" section */}
  <div>
    <h2 className="text-lg font-semibold mb-3">My Overview</h2>
    <div className="grid gap-4 grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3">
      <MyActiveProjects />
      <MyOpenBids />
      <MyTodo />
    </div>
  </div>

  {/* Company-Level Widgets - KPI section */}
  <div>
    <h2 className="text-lg font-semibold mb-3">Company Overview</h2>
    <div className="grid gap-4 grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-4">
      <TotalActiveProjects />
      <CompanyOpenBids />
      <PendingPayApps />
      <BidOpportunities />
    </div>
  </div>

  {/* Financial Widgets */}
  <div>
    <h2 className="text-lg font-semibold mb-3">Financial Summary</h2>
    <div className="grid gap-4 grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3">
      <RevenueInProgress />
      <Backlog />
      <EstimatingPipeline />
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
```

**Key design decisions:**
- Use `space-y-6` (slightly tighter than previous `space-y-8`) for Procore-style compact density
- Section headers (`h2`) group widgets logically: "My Overview", "Company Overview", "Financial Summary"
- Responsive grid: 1 col mobile, 2 cols tablet (sm), 3-4 cols desktop (lg)
- User widgets in row of 3, company count widgets in row of 4, financial widgets in row of 3
- No decorative elements, animations, or borders between sections — data-first design

**The safety net logic (useEffect for org activation) must remain** since it handles the critical case where a user's session has no org but they have organizations available. Keep the `organizations` query and the `isActivating` state.
